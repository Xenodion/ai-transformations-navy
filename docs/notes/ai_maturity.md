## Pilars of AI maturity readiness

- **Business startegy :** How AI supports business goals

- **Technology and data strategy :** Data and infrastrcutre to fuel and run IA solutions at scale

- **AI strategy and experience :** AI expertise and repeatable processes to create sustainable value

- **Organization & Culture :** Leadership vision, operating model, skills

- **AI Governance :** Processes, controls and accountability structures to govern data privacy, security and responsible use of AI


## AI readiness stages

- **Exploring (value 0 /cost 0) :** starting out with AI

- **Planning (value 0 /cost $) :** actively assessing, defining, and planning an AI strategy

- **Implementing (value $ /cost $$$) :** Bulid and run

- **Scaling (value $$$$$ /cost $$$$) :** Scaling AI projects across the organization

- **Realizing (Ai by design) :** Realizing repeatable and measurable value across the organization.
