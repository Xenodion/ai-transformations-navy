# Reserve candidates: concise project estimates

**Status:** First-pass assumptions for discussion, 10 November 2026. The four featured cases must be reviewed before these candidates are selected or funded. The figures here are not company forecasts, quotes or verified cash returns. All candidate estimates refer to incremental activity beyond existing capabilities and the continuation baseline. None are automatically included in the committed programme.

Amounts below are **USD million** except where noted. The five-year annual model in `../finance/portfolio_model_14_revised.xlsx` uses each project's hurdle from `../finance/discount_rate_policy.md`, a hypothetical 25% tax rate on positive operating surplus, five-year depreciation of assumed CAPEX, and no immediate tax credit for losses. Dates, accounting treatment, tax, economic ownership, operational baselines and cost estimates require validation. The workbook sums the 14 as an illustrative scenario; overlaps must be resolved before the sum can be used as an investment decision.

**Scenario convention [Assumption]:** downside uses 90% benefits and 105% initial and operating costs; base uses the stated amounts; upside uses 115% benefits and base costs. P12 stops after Y2 in the downside and retains an assumed $0.10m Y3 exit cost. A separate severe stress uses 65% benefits and 120% costs. These are illustrative scenarios, not verified frequencies.

## Shared cash-flow convention and decision limits

The model schedules capital expenditure in Y0–Y1 and one-time implementation in Y1. Each case has a five-year benefit ramp and recurring operating costs recorded in the executable source. The one-time investment in the table excludes recurring Y1–Y5 costs, tax and any terminal value. The assumptions are narrower pilot scopes than the earlier broad long-list figures; those earlier numbers are not evidence or an automatic basis for scaling. Each estimate needs the relevant cost and operational owner to check feasibility, vendor pricing, baseline and incremental cash before funding.

P07 is a safety-led candidate whose avoided risk is excluded from cash by default; a non-financial safety decision requires its own explicit governance and is not counted as hard cash. P10 does not pass the illustrative base and requires redesign, a changed scope or rejection. P12 is an H3 research option with no fabricated payback. E01 is a shared programme cost. These decisions are not a license to hide costs or force projects to clear the hurdle.

Source context and already-existing capabilities: [long list](long_list.md), `sources/register.md` CLM-017 to CLM-027, CLM-060/061 and CLM-065 to CLM-068; the per-use-case quantities, rates, benefit lifts and project costs in this table are assumptions unless separately validated.
