# Complementary project cards

Working estimates, 11 November 2026. These five business cases complement the four featured cases. Monetary values are USD millions and are scenario assumptions, not company results. The model uses Y0–Y5, 25% illustrative tax on positive operating surplus, straight-line depreciation of assumed CAPEX, and no immediate tax credit on losses. The [working workbook](../finance/portfolio_model_working.xlsx) contains annual costs, ramps and cash flows.

## P04 — Renewable fleet performance (H1)

**Proposal.** Improve detection and resolution of avoidable faults across eligible renewable assets, using existing monitoring and the Digital Factory's capabilities. Start with a pilot, then extend only where asset owners can demonstrate incremental generation over the existing O3C, OEM and local maintenance baseline. Operations retain dispatch and maintenance authority.

**Base annual benefit at full scale.** An assumed 3,000 MW of eligible assets × 8,760 h × 30% capacity factor × 0.45% additional generation × $45/MWh net value = **$1.5965m/year**. This is an illustrative attributable generation improvement, not 0.45 percentage points of capacity factor. Check whether output is actually saleable, the group's economic interest, PPA curtailment and price, OEM guarantees and whether benefits are already captured. Avoid double counting existing forecast and trading improvements.

**Costs.** One-time spending $1.40m: $0.30m CAPEX in Y0, $0.30m CAPEX and $0.80m launch expense in Y1. Recurring costs: $0.25m / $0.30m / $0.25m / $0.25m / $0.25m in Y1–Y5; total gross spending **$2.70m**. Includes the assumed integration, delivery and support envelope; confirm supplier terms, site staff and backfill. Base rollout fractions: 20% / 50% / 80% / 100% / 100%.

**Scenarios.** Downside assumes 80% of full-scale benefit, 110% of costs and rollout of 15% / 40% / 70% / 90% / 100%; upside assumes 115% benefit and an accelerated ramp, at base costs. At an assumed 10% hurdle, downside/base/upside NPV is **−$0.035m / +$1.054m / +$1.773m**; IRR is **9.2% / 36.3% / 54.7%**; simple payback is **4.34 / 3.12 / 2.57 years**. Confirm a matched baseline and measured, sold generation before scaling.

## P08 — Electricity and gas customer retention (H1)

**Proposal.** Help service teams identify customers likely to leave and offer suitable support, with human supervision and consent/marketing controls. Compare targeting with current retention practices and simpler rules; existing customer assistants and CRM remain in the baseline.

**Base annual benefit at full scale.** An assumed **1.2 million eligible accounts × 10% baseline annual churn × 9% relative churn reduction × $150 annual contribution = $1.62m/year**. A 9% relative reduction takes assumed churn from 10% to 9.1%, a 0.9-percentage-point improvement. The $150 is assumed annual retained contribution after incremental incentives and service costs, not revenue. Use a controlled comparison and count each customer's incremental contribution once; independently validate eligible population, churn, margin, offer cost and attrition.

**Costs.** One-time spending $1.50m: $0.30m CAPEX in Y0, $0.30m CAPEX and $0.90m launch expense in Y1. Recurring costs: $0.20m / $0.25m / $0.30m / $0.35m / $0.35m in Y1–Y5; total gross spending **$2.95m**. Includes assumed customer-data integration, delivery, model operation and change costs; confirm CRM contracts, privacy controls and team capacity. Base rollout fractions: 20% / 50% / 80% / 100% / 100%.

**Scenarios.** Downside assumes 80% of full-scale benefit, 110% of costs and rollout of 15% / 40% / 70% / 90% / 100%; upside assumes 115% benefit and an accelerated ramp, at base costs. At an assumed 9.5% hurdle, downside/base/upside NPV is **−$0.139m / +$0.992m / +$1.732m**; IRR is **6.2% / 34.1% / 52.8%**; simple payback is **4.52 / 3.15 / 2.57 years**. Expand only when a control group demonstrates incremental net retention and Finance confirms customer economics.

## P02 — Upstream surveillance (H1)

Two eligible production hubs × 135 barrels/day × 365 × $15/barrel contribution = **$1.47825m/year** at full scale. One-time investment is $0.90m and gross Y0–Y5 cost is $2.23m. Confirm sales, rights, existing surveillance and distinct events from drilling P03.

## P05 — Copilot licence and workflow redesign (H1)

10,000 paid licences × 12% permanently cancelled × $30/month × 12 = **$0.432m/year**. One-time investment is $0.30m and gross Y0–Y5 cost is $0.73m. Confirm cancelability and necessary functionality; no staff time is monetized.

## P09 — LNG voyage optimisation (H2)

30 eligible voyages × $1m controllable fuel cost × 2% incremental saving = **$0.60m/year**. One-time investment is $0.50m and gross Y0–Y5 cost is $1.04m. Confirm contract rights, existing routing and actual fuel-payment attribution.
