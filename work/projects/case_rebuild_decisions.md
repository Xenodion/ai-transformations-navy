# Revised project assumptions for financial reconstruction

**Status:** Working decision record for the revised model. These do not establish an approved budget or a forecast. The [P01](case_p01_refining_pdm.md), [P03](case_p03_deepwater_drilling.md), [P11](case_p11_virtual_power_plant.md) and [P13](case_p13_retail_station_demand.md) cards now contain the active detailed cases, annual cash and J-curves; the current financial values and scenarios come from `../finance/portfolio_model_14_revised.xlsx`. All amounts below are USD unless otherwise stated.

## Shared comparison and controls

The counterfactual includes TotalEnergies' existing digital teams, contracts, asset programmes and savings plan. Incremental value is the difference between the proposed programme and that counterfactual, not the entire value of an existing platform. Record benefits only once, net of ownership, partner entitlement and variable costs. Quantities and rates not documented for the eligible perimeter remain **Unknown**; do not silently substitute zero for missing evidence when evaluating a full business case. Zero revenue in a conservative scenario is a scenario assumption, not a claim that an initiative has no value.

Each case needs a dated budget covering the six course cost categories: compute, talent, integration, model operations, data and governance. Show fixed and variable elements, adoption/training, procurement and non-cancellable commitments; distinguish incremental external cash from internal capacity. Classify accounting CAPEX and OPEX only after checking the actual expenditure. Apply taxes and working capital on a consistent post-tax basis before comparing NPV with a post-tax hurdle. Funded exploratory costs remain in programme cash even when commercial option proceeds are unquantified.

## P01 — Refining predictive maintenance

**Decision:** Retain as a candidate H1 pilot, not an approved $15m/year forecast. Select the refinery and equipment population after checking installed solutions, ownership and data access. Normal planned maintenance and existing AspenTech deployments form part of the counterfactual (CLM-018).

**Benefit bridge:** incremental production sold × site-specific contribution margin per barrel, limited by market demand and site constraints, plus documented repair invoices avoided, with no overlap with P10. Each input is **Unknown** until established at the same sites and in the same period. The historical 1m b/d × 0.55% × $7.1/b × 365 = $14.25m is arithmetic on assumptions; a market refining marker is not proof of the site's incremental margin or of the eligible throughput. The Equinor $120m since 2020 is a cumulative company-reported value including partners (SRC-017); the $20m/year and per-machine rate are derived averages with restrictive assumptions, not an observed annual result or validation of P01.

**Gate:** Site reliability lead and finance agree a baseline, data quality, alert burden and intervention verification before launch. Measure avoided downtime and realized cash against the agreed counterfactual; define alert thresholds after historical back-testing and before the live trial. Safety events lead to an immediate operational pause and independent review. A later investment is decided on future incremental cash and risks, not on sunk pilot expenditure. Dates and numerical thresholds are **Unknown** pending site data.

## P03 — Deepwater well-planning and advisory

**Decision:** Retain as a candidate H2 workflow redesign, conditional on an identified operator, rig schedule, contractor agreement and safety review. H2 classification is provisional and must be tested on the specific workflow, not selected to balance a chart. Mero is operated by Petrobras; the 2025 TotalEnergies announcement lists its 19.3% interest (SRC-037 / CLM-077). The old claim that Mero is TotalEnergies-operated is rejected.

**Benefit bridge:** eligible contracted rig-days avoided × the contractually avoidable cash cost per day × TotalEnergies' actual entitlement, minus implementation and incentive costs, with reductions to days and rate applied only once. A gross day-rate multiplied by time is not automatically a cash saving if the charter charge continues; unused capacity and improved schedule are reported separately. **Unknown:** eligible rig count, baseline drilling plan, contract flexibility, partner approvals, data rights, net entitlement and the amount incremental to the $12.5bn savings programme. Until established, the $35m/year extrapolation is rejected as a base-case cash input.

**Gate:** Authorised operator, drilling owner, HSE and finance confirm data access, contractor incentives, a comparison group and an auditable invoice or other cash mechanism before a live trial. Stop or pause if safety or permission conditions are not met; retain sunk and non-cancellable charges. Performance thresholds and timing require a campaign-specific plan and cannot be inferred from annual cash flows.

## P11 — Customer-side flexibility option

**Decision:** Keep only as a staged exploratory option until the team demonstrates what is new relative to Quadra and existing commercial offers. In 2023 Quadra's announced VPP aggregated 9 GW of renewable generation (SRC-036 / CLM-076); this does not establish or exclude the proposed EV and battery service. H3 is provisional; apply the course's customer, business model, technology and timing questions to the defined offer.

**Financial treatment:** no P11 commercial receipts, NPV, IRR or payback are reported as a validated project forecast. Approved learning expenditures and unavoidable exit costs belong in the whole-programme cash flow. A future conditional commercial illustration must use consistent enrolled MW, availability, settlement rights, customer payments and costs; label it outside the funded base case. The earlier $6m at 100 MW is incompatible with the stated $12k–$20k/MW/year assumptions and is withdrawn.

**Learning gate:** define the eligible customers and country, existing offers and data rights; then test enrollment cost, controllable capacity, dispatch performance, opt-outs and net settled margin. Operational and regulatory checks precede any live dispatch. Thresholds, dates and required funding are **Unknown** until agreed with the relevant operator and finance owner. Failed trials retain sunk and contractual termination costs.

## P13 — Retail replenishment

**Decision (10 November):** Vincent suggests approximately **€1 million** for a hypothetical pilot at **100 large French company-operated stations**, including electronic shelf labels (CLM-078). This is calibration guidance informed by local experience, not evidence of an actual project or verified quote; exact costs and scope remain assumptions. Use 100 sites and make any broader rollout conditional on pilot economics. The earlier 180-site illustration and old 800- or 1,500-site budgets are superseded.

**Payback objective [Assumption]:** Vincent suggests **2–3 years** as a plausible target for the hypothetical proposal (CLM-079), not a measured outcome. Calculate both simple and discounted payback and distinguish investment, ongoing cost, tax and incremental cash.

**Benefit bridge:** compare (A) forecasting and replenishment to reduce waste and excess stock with (B) forecasting plus electronic labels and dynamic markdowns. Count waste reduction at inventory cost and the cash effect of stock-out recovery or working-capital release only when documented; count dynamic markdown recovery only for items not already credited to waste reduction. Quantify product category, seasonal baseline, store sales, inventory cost, measured lift and adoption on the same 100-site perimeter. The old 180-site $607,500 sales-value calculation and the $2.5m annual total are superseded for this pilot; neither is verified cash.

**Cost and gate:** use the indicative **~€1 million total pilot investment including labels** as the starting observation for option B, not as an additive software-only budget. Fixed/variable allocation, accounting CAPEX/OPEX, recurring costs, contingency and phasing are unknown. Cost option A separately, with no labels only if the actual workflow permits it; do not count label savings and forecasting benefits twice. The former $2.1104m/180-site sensitivity is superseded and not the pilot estimate. Before rollout, establish a baseline and comparable sites, seasonality controls, inventory ownership and a minimum detectable effect. Agree thresholds and duration before launch; an exit retains expenditure already incurred and non-cancellable vendor costs.

## E01 and reserve candidates

The value register is a governance cost and measurement mechanism. Do not count its previous $5.5m annual benefit as separate cash without an independently attributed baseline and no overlap with projects. P05 time savings, P07 safety benefits and P06 procurement gains are non-cash or unquantified until cash realization, attribution and eligible scope are documented. Retain all candidates in the long list, but do not include their costs or benefits in the committed case before an explicit release; show any reserve authorization ceiling separately from spending.
