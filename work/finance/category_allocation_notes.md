# Indicative cost-category bridge

`category_allocation.py` reads gross annual model costs and apportions each year top-down across the six cost classes. This is a **classification assumption**, not a transaction-level or project-level cost estimate. Shares by category, in the order compute and equipment / talent and change / integration / model operations / data engineering / governance and legal, are: Y0 30/25/20/5/15/5%; Y1 20/25/25/8/17/5%; Y2 20/25/15/17/16/7%; Y3 18/24/12/22/16/8%. They are illustrative and require replacement with project purchase orders, staff allocations and cost-category mapping before commitments. The early compute category includes physical hardware and software, notably P13 labels; talent includes operational backfill and training. Existing lines may combine several categories. No category is an incremental cost, and no project-level six-category composition is implied. The figures sum to the model's existing Y0–Y3 annual gross costs before rounding; Y0 is shown separately because the model has an upfront period. The six-category bridge supplements, and does not replace, the four focus-case cost breakdown. Use Y1–Y3 as the three-year operating view and Y0–Y3 for upfront plus three-year total.
