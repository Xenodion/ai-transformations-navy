## B. Team choices that can be made without internal company data

1. **Business-case assumptions:** choose ranges and justify them as assumptions. Do not wait for real TotalEnergies quotes or internal contracts to submit an academic proposal. The gate then makes actual validation a condition of cash release.
2. **Cost of inaction:** if package 1 lacks a credible independent driver, use the portfolio's **foregone incremental NPV** as the quantified opportunity cost and explain why an added do-nothing erosion amount would double count or invent risk. Do not force different “without/with” numbers.
3. **P07/P10:** keep both in the programme and financial ceiling. P07 is a safety-led test with no booked incident cash; P10 is industrial learning with a negative current cash case. State the strategic rationale and refuse automatic scale until its own evidence gate passes.
4. **Hurdles:** retain the published **7% post-tax impairment WACC proxy** as a reference, plus the already documented team branch/project premiums. The optional 10% common portfolio screening rate must be described as a separate team assumption, not a TotalEnergies hurdle or an average of project rates.
5. **Ownership:** use accountable **roles** (segment sponsor, operations lead, Finance signatory, Digital Line/Factory lead, HSE/legal/cyber) unless genuine named owners are known. A fictional internal appointment would weaken the report.
