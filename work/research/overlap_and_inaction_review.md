No invoice, site ledger, customer list, contract or internal programme register was available to verify the boundaries. A proposed partition makes overlap testable; it does not prove benefits are additive.

## Consequence for the decision

The report's +$1.571m present value of assumed avoided inaction, including the market and future-offer pools above, is **conditional** on all applicable exclusions and funded intervention mechanisms. The source evidence does not identify an eligible $150m pool or establish that the programme avoids 50% of its losses. If an overlap or causal link cannot be demonstrated, remove the affected component and recalculate; a zero-avoidance case yields the original portfolio NPV and a negative downside at the common hurdle. Do not re-label the adjusted positive downside as an independently verified forecast.
