## 3. Vulnerabilities during Oral Defense (4 Points of CEO Attack)

During the oral presentation, CEO Patrick Pouyanné (played by the professor) will probe the following vulnerabilities:

### Attack 1: The "Frontline Champion Ransom" Challenge
- **CEO Pushback:** *"You require the Business Champion to dedicate 20% to 40% of protected, ring-fenced time inside the Agile Trio. On a deepwater rig in Angola or during a turnaround at the Normandy steam cracker, my engineers are already working 60-hour weeks fighting operational fires. Who is doing their operational job while they build AI models? What prevents the branch manager from pulling the champion out of the trio the minute an operating hiccup occurs?"*
- **Defense Gap in Document:** The document asserts 20%–40% protected time and branch co-funding, but does not provide an enforceable organizational safeguard (e.g., tying branch management incentives to Trio delivery milestones, or funding a backfill deputy during the sprint).

### Attack 2: The H3 Real Option Return-Mix Paradox (Rule 7 Tension)
- **CEO Pushback:** *"In Section 4, you state that P11 (Virtual Power Plant) is an H3 Real Option governed strictly by learning gates, where we can terminate at Month 18 if milestones are missed. Yet in Table 1, you bank $98.0M of 5-year cumulative value from P11 and fold it directly into your headline $338.5M economic return! You cannot claim real option conservatism while simultaneously treating unproven Y4–Y5 commercial scale-up as committed cash flows in your Comex summary."*
- **Defense Gap in Document:** AGENTS.md Rule 7 specifies: *"Use horizon-specific metrics. Do not fabricate a payback for an H3 option."* While P11 does not fabricate an IRR/payback, bundling $98.0M of H3 potential into the same cumulative total as audited H1/H2 operational savings blurs the line between committed cash flows and unrisked option upside.

### Attack 3: Double-Counting with the $12.5bn Cost-Savings Plan (`CLM-007`)
- **CEO Pushback:** *"We announced a $12.5bn group cost-savings plan for 2026–2030 (CLM-007). My operating directors have already committed to capex and opex reductions across E&P and refining. How do I know your $15.0M/yr in refining maintenance (P01) and $35.0M/yr in drilling optimization (P03) are not already counted in that $12.5bn plan? Are you double-counting my own targets back to me?"*
- **Defense Gap in Document:** The document rightfully flags this risk for P06 (procurement), but must explicitly state how Enabler E01 establishes an incremental, audited baseline for P01 and P03 that separates AI-induced uptime from standard plant efficiency programs.

### Attack 4: Thin-Slice Foundation Dependency Latency
- **CEO Pushback:** *"You say data infrastructure is already funded, but AspenTech Inmation and Cognite rollouts extend to 2027 and 2028. If the data pipeline for the Atapu rig or the Antwerp site is delayed by 6 months, does your Agile Trio sit idle charging overhead?"*
- **Defense Gap in Document:** The document needs to explicitly specify the "offline data thin-slice" protocol (e.g., historical CSV/OSIsoft PI extracts used for initial offline model training and validation while production pipelines are established).
