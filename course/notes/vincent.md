# Vincent's notes — oral expectations and working choices

> Reformulated from Vincent's notes and his clarifications on 7 November 2026. These notes supplement the written guidelines. They are neither public evidence about TotalEnergies nor verbatim quotations from the professor. Check the course materials where there is a discrepancy.

## Reported expectations from the professor

- **Speak to executives:** make the CAPEX/OPEX split immediately clear and provide justified orders of magnitude. Benchmarks help only when their exact source and comparability are checked.
- **Forecast horizon:** three years is the usual frame; five years may be justified for TotalEnergies. Explain the choice and show cash flows by year.
- **Numerical rigor:** TotalEnergies publishes extensive data, so the professor can challenge our claims. Source facts, make calculations reproducible, and identify assumptions required by the fictional exercise.
- **Level of detail:** retain calculations and references for Q&A; show only decision-relevant numbers in the pitch.
- **Competition:** compare competitors' choices and measured outcomes on comparable scopes, then explain the implication for the decision maker.
- **Format:** 15-minute presentation and 5-minute Q&A. Outputs include the slide deck and memo.

## Vincent's working choices

- Let research shape the strategy; revise hypotheses when evidence contradicts them. Do not collect only supporting evidence.
- Avoid claiming to judge the entire data strategy of a group this large from outside. Identify contestable choices and observable consequences, then propose justified alternatives.
- Experience at Argedis points to possible operational friction. It is a **local observation**, requiring broader corroboration before any group-level conclusion.
- Write the full report, derive the executive memo, then build the slides. Consider about ten projects, fully analyze only three or four representative cases, and estimate the others more roughly.
- A bubble chart can show the portfolio using potential gain, payback, cost, and horizon color, with two stacked bars for investment mix and expected-return mix. Vincent recalls this from the professor's whiteboard; the axes were not found as a written PDF requirement.
- Vincent reports an **evening of 10 November 2026** submission deadline, five days before the defense. Structural changes should end before submission; oral preparation may continue.

## Verify before presenting as fact

- Source and comparability of each financial or competitor benchmark.
- Whether H3 projects have a defensible payback; otherwise show learning gates and option value without inventing a return.
- Exact submission format and channel for the full report, memo, and slides.
