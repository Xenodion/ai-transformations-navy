# Project / oral exam guidelines and information

## Selected company

Team 4, composed of Alexandre Waerniers, Vincent Lamy, Yoni Mahieddine and Nahel Bachriri, selected **TotalEnergies**.

## Format

- **Date**: 16 November 2026.
- **Weight**: 30% of the course grade (the written exam is the other 70%).
- **Length**: 20 minutes, 15 min pitch + 5 min Q&A.
- **Role play**: the professor plays the CEO of the company. We present **and defend** our AI strategy under sceptical questioning.

## Company rules

Each group is free to choose its company, with these rules:

- 1 company = 1 group. Two groups cannot cover the same company.
- The company cannot be a tech company (such as Nvidia, Google or Microsoft).
- The company must be large enough for an AI strategy to make sense: at least €1bn in turnover and at least 2,000 employees.

## What the pitch must cover

The pitch should cover every topic seen in class, following the course logic: diagnose readiness → pick the operating model → sequence the portfolio → prove it financially → manage the change.

| # | Deliverable | Course frameworks to use | Session |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Readiness assessment, naming the **bottleneck** | 5 maturity pillars, 5 readiness stages | 1 |
| 2 | Operating-model recommendation | CoE / hub-and-spoke / federated, 3 matching factors (size, regulation, talent), decision tree, evolution path | 2 |
| 3 | Preliminary portfolio one-pager | Three Horizons, 70-20-10, dependency graph, build/buy/blend, sequencing canvas | 2 |
| 4 | Financial analysis: investment strategy and return | ROI, payback, NPV, IRR, hurdle rate | 3 |
| 5 | Change-management approach for success | Change management and executive engagement | 4 |

For details on each framework, see the cheat sheets in [exam/](exam/).

### Portfolio one-pager: the sequencing canvas

One row per initiative:

| Initiative | Horizon | Capital % | Depends on | Build / buy / partner | First milestone | Owner and date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

- The capital column sums to 100%. If the split moves away from 70-20-10, state the trigger and the reason.
- Dependencies name a **slice** of a foundation, not the whole platform ("a slice, not a lake").
- Each foundation (data, governance, skills) is funded with its first use case.

## Teacher's advice

- Come prepared to be challenged against your competitors.
- Use financial numbers to back up your strategy.
- Behind each number, you must be able to explain how it was created.
- Hypotheses are fine, but the methodology behind them must be strong.
- Compare scenarios (worst, base, best) **and** calculate the cost of doing nothing.
