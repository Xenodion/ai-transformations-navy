### 5. Facts that might challenge the perceived need for change

- **Strong financial position.** ROACE 12.6% (claimed best among the majors for four years), production costs below $5/boe, gearing 9.1% at 30 Sep 2026, and a record Downstream quarter in Q3 2026 (SRC-003, SRC-005). The CEO will not accept "the company is in trouble" as a reason to invest.
- **AI is already funded and organised.** There is a Digital Factory, a Digital Line created in Sept 2025, and data-platform rollouts with Cognite and AspenTech (see [2026-11-08_totalenergies_ai_data_organization.md](2026-11-08_totalenergies_ai_data_organization.md)). Our proposal must add value on top of this, not reinvent it.
- **Pressures that remain.** The savings target rose by $5bn in five months (SRC-003 vs SRC-004). The Board planned for 2026 to "review the assessment of the impact of technological disruptions, including artificial intelligence" (SRC-002, p. 225). Risk factor §3.1.5 names AI adoption pace as a risk (SRC-002, p. 138).

## Contradictions and unknowns

- **Savings plan, $7.5bn vs $12.5bn.** The Sept 2025 figure is superseded by the Feb 2026 figure, which covers the same period, 2026–2030. Use $12.5bn. Status: `Superseded`.
- **Middle East impact, ~15% (March 2026) vs ~5% (Q3 2026).** These probably reflect a gradual restart ("gradual return-to-normal oil production in the Middle East", SRC-005, §3.3) rather than a conflict, but the March primary text is unread. Check: find the official March release text on totalenergies.com or SEC (Form 6-K).
- **Training spend, €220M vs €210M** (SRC-002, pp. 6 and 13). Minor.
- **Unknowns:**
  - The levers behind the $12.5bn savings plan: how much is opex, and whether digital or AI contributes.
  - Any AI-specific budget.
  - Production and employees by segment.
  - Audited 2025 revenue (only the $201bn infographic figure was checked).
  - The 2026 sensitivity table (SRC-003, p. 13). Its layout is scrambled in text extraction, so it is not used.
- **Conclusions the sources do not support:** none of these documents establishes TotalEnergies' AI maturity, a performance gap versus competitors, or the value of any AI use case.
