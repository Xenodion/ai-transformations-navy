Disconfirming tests: an already-funded internal equivalent; partner restrictions on IP; no measurable advantage over conventional experiments; no third-party willingness to pay; or a scope limited to internal production optimization. Any of these defeats the current H3 thesis. Research duration alone never establishes H3.

The retained $2.85m is a planning ceiling inherited from the catalyst experiment, not a vendor quote or a commercial plant budget. It buys feasibility and laboratory learning only. Commercialization requires a separate business case and funding.
